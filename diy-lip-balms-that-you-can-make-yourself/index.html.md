# How to Make DIY Lip Balm at Home: 8 Easy Natural Recipes

In your quest to rid your skincare routine of harsh synthetic chemicals, creating your own natural beauty products is a rewarding first step. Learning how to make lip balm without vaseline or heavy petroleum additives gives you complete control over what touches your skin. Whether you want to craft a nourishing overnight salve, a classic protective stick, or a homemade lip scrub with vaseline-free organic ingredients, these DIY lip balm recipes feature gentle oils, rich waxes, and delicious natural flavors. They are quick, fun, and surprisingly simple to craft right in your kitchen.

Before diving into the recipes, here is a quick overview of popular natural ingredients and their primary benefits:

| Ingredient | Primary Benefit | Best For |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Beeswax | Locks in moisture and creates a protective seal | Daily lip protection |
| Coconut Oil | Deeply hydrates and softens dry skin | Chapped or sensitive lips |
| Shea Butter | Delivers intense vitamins and healing fats | Overnight lip repair |

Here are the essential supplies you'll want to gather for your homemade lip balm setup:

- Empty lip balm tubes or small reusable tin containers

- Wooden toothpicks or glass stirrers

- A small heat-safe funnel for spill-free pouring

- A double boiler or glass bowl set over simmering water

## 1. Start Simple

_ For those who want a minimalist, no-frills DIY lip balm, this two-ingredient formula is the perfect starting point. Basically, this beauty recipe calls only for beeswax and coconut oil, forming a gentle protective barrier that keeps moisture locked in all day. Melt equal parts together over low heat before transferring to containers. Other materials that you will need are empty tubes where you can put your finished product, toothpicks, a funnel, and a double boiler.

Source: woodsofbelltrees.com_

## 2. Add Your Favorite Essential Oils

![food,dessert,petal,produce,heart,](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/kp/g2/cx9le_food_dessert_petal_produce_heart_600x532.jpg)

Building on our [simple two-ingredient formula](#1), this custom recipe calls for a couple more nourishing ingredients for extra hydration. Apart from beeswax and coconut oil, soothing shea butter and a few drops of therapeutic essential oil like lavender or peppermint are added. A note on supplies: make sure that you use items or tools that are specifically designated for your cosmetic-making adventures to keep everything hygienic.

Source: [wecanmakeanything.net](http://www.wecanmakeanything.net/2015/02/diy-lip-balm.html)

## 3. Sprinkle in Some Honey

![food,produce,flavor,](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/z8/kl/ykgv3_food_produce_flavor_600x400.jpg)

Here is another DIY lip balm recipe that requires only a short list of natural ingredients. You will need beeswax, coconut oil, and raw organic honey for this fun and simple recipe. Honey acts as a natural humectant that attracts moisture to dry lips. You will also need a few drops of vitamin E oil for antioxidant skin protection, making it ideal if you are looking for how to make lip balm with beetroot and honey or natural oils.

Source: [dreamalittlebigger.com](https://www.dreamalittlebigger.com/post/beeswax-honey-lip-balm.html)

## 4. Add Natural Coloring

![,](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/mt/dx/y8fxwpvl5542d2446c80b984792114.jpg__600x391.jpg)

If you want your lip balm to give your lips a subtle, rosy tint, adding a natural plant pigment elevates your creation. Unlike synthetic colorants, you can go completely natural by using organic beetroot powder. We recommend sourcing your powder from a reputable aromatherapy supplier with verified organic merchandise. You can also make your own beetroot powder by dehydrating thin beet slices, though keep in mind there might be a short trial and error phase to get the pigment smooth.

Source: [blahblahmagazine.com.au](http://blahblahmagazine.com.au/homemade-tinted-lip-balm/)

## 5. Make It Floral

_ If you loved adding custom fragrances like [essential oil blends](#2), this floral variation offers a lovely botanical twist. This recipe calls for dried hibiscus petals infused directly into warm base oils. You will find that there is a wide array of dried flowers—such as calendula or rose petals—that you can use when crafting custom herbal lip salves. Aside from the dried flowers, this recipe relies on beeswax, coconut oil, and sweet orange essential oil for an uplifting scent.

Source: woodsofbelltrees.com_

## 6. Make Your Own Chapstick

![medicine,](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/ws/vf/nw92f_medicine_600x401.jpg)

This classic, ultra-moisturizing formulation focuses entirely on skin repair without added tints or strong fragrances. It is fantastic for protecting your lips against harsh winds and dry winter weather. While [naturally tinted beetroot balms](#4) add color, this pure chapstick uses rich cocoa butter and olive oil blended with beeswax and coconut oil to deliver long-lasting relief for cracked lips.

Source: [littlehomebyhand.wordpress.com](https://littlehomebyhand.wordpress.com/2011/10/13/making-chapstick/)

## 7. Yes, You Can Add Chocolate, Too

![leaf,branch,jewellery,flower,produce,](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/z9/p4/q0r5w_leaf_branch_jewellery_flower_produce_600x400.jpg)

For those who want a lip balm that feels like a sweet decadent treat, this recipe is a must-try. Complementing the rich cocoa butter featured in our [protective chapstick blend](#6), you can stir food-grade dark chocolate chips directly into the melted wax and oil mixture. A few drops of pure vanilla extract will go a long way in creating a delicious chocolate-vanilla aroma that keeps your lips feeling soft and smelling amazing.

Source: [hellonatural.co](https://helloglow.co/honey-lip-balm-lip-scrub/)

## 8. Make It Minty

![food,dish,plant,produce,citrus,](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/g5/xv/es27r_food_dish_plant_produce_citrus_520x346.jpg)

Spearmint and peppermint remain two of the most popular refreshing flavors for lip balms. Since there are so many fun, clean recipes available for DIY mint-flavored lip balms, we suggest avoiding synthetic store-bought options that may contain artificial preservatives. Making your own batch takes less than an hour, leaving you with a fresh, cooling balm that naturally energizes your senses.

Source: [afreshlegacy.net](https://goo.gl/C8cFsa)

Go ahead and discard your old store-bought tubes or plastic tubs. Grab some beeswax, carrier oils, and your favorite natural botanicals to enjoy creating your very own signature blend of lip balm. Who knows—you might just inspire your friends or launch your own organic beauty line!
