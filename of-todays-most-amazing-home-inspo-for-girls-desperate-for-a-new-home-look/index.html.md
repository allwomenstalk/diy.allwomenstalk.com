# 23 Best Home Decor Instagram Accounts for Endless Interior Design Inspo

If you feel uninspired by your current living space, giving your home a fresh look does not have to require a complete renovation. Updating your home decor with thoughtful styling choices, cozy textiles, and curated aesthetic elements can instantly transform any room. To help you curate your dream space, we have rounded up the top interior design and home styling accounts on Instagram that offer endless daily inspiration.

Before diving into the top accounts, here are a few quick tips for refreshing your home aesthetic on any budget:

- **Mix Textures:** Combine linen, natural wood, leather, and woven fabrics to add depth and warmth to your rooms.
- **Focus on Lighting:** Layer ambient, task, and accent lighting with stylish lamps and statement fixtures.
- **Incorporate Greenery:** Add indoor plants or high-quality botanicals for vibrant natural energy.

## 1. @ashleytstark

Ashley Stark Kenner, Creative Director of Stark Carpet, shares breathtaking high-end luxury interiors, architectural marvels, and sophisticated rug styling. Her feed is a masterclass in elegant texture combination and neutral color palettes. If you love refined, upscale interiors, her posts will inspire your next living room upgrade. For more architectural inspiration, check out [clean modern styling ideas](#22) below.

## 2. @decoristofficial

Decorist highlights accessible online interior design concepts that make stylish living attainable for every budget. From chic nursery makeovers to cozy living room layouts, their feed offers practical ideas you can easily replicate in your own home.

## 3. @brookewagnerdesign

Brooke Wagner Design showcases coastal contemporary aesthetic at its finest. With light-filled open rooms, natural wood finishes, and soothing neutral hues, this account proves how impactful subtle contrast and airy layouts can be for modern living spaces.

## 4. @auslandinteriors

Ausland Interiors delivers bespoke residential design with a focus on custom millwork, rich textures, and timeless kitchen designs. Their attention to subtle design details creates rooms that feel both lived-in and luxurious.

## 5. @luluandgeorgia

From statement rugs to artisanal furniture pieces, [Lulu and Georgia](https://www.luluandgeorgia.com) brings designer flair directly to home decor enthusiasts. Their feed highlights trendy statement lighting, earthy tones, and curated lounge setups that elevate any interior.

## 6. @scoutandnimble

Scout & Nimble blends warm minimalist decor with functional living solutions. Their posts frequently feature curated accent pillows, handcrafted pottery, and cozy seating arrangements that make home decorating feel effortless.

## 7. @wisteriahomedecor

Wisteria is known for eclectic, globally inspired home furnishings and vintage-inspired decor pieces. If you want to introduce unique character and story-driven accents into your rooms, this account offers constant inspiration.

## 8. @homedeconl

HomeDeco NL brings crisp European minimalism and Scandinavian design principles into focus. Expect clean monochromatic schemes, sleek furniture silhouettes, and subtle greenery arrangements that celebrate modern simplicity.

Here is a quick style breakdown of popular decor aesthetics to help you pinpoint your ideal vibe:

| Style Category | Key Design Elements | Best For |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Coastal Modern | Light woods, linen fabrics, soft neutrals | Open, breezy living spaces |
| Warm Minimalism | Earthy tones, organic shapes, minimal clutter | Relaxing bedrooms and lounges |
| Modern Traditional | Classic moldings, brass hardware, layered rugs | Timeless home renovations |

## 9. @selamatdesigns

Selamat Designs specializes in handcrafted rattan, wicker, and tropical-inspired furniture pieces. Their creations infuse warmth and natural textures into indoor living areas and covered outdoor patios alike.

## 10. @mydomaine

MyDomaine is a premier online design publication sharing expert decorating advice, house tours, and organizing hacks. Whether you are re-styling a small space or embarking on a full remodel, their feed is filled with actionable guidance. Compare their tips with [budget apartment decor solutions](#21) for more practical ideas.

## 11. @pencilandpaperco

Pencil & Paper Co. is famous for vibrant color mixing, cheerful pattern play, and classic Southern charm. If you want to step away from neutral palettes and bring energetic colors into your home, this account is a must-follow.

## 12. @innerdecor

Inner Decor highlights creative interior setups, clever space-saving arrangements, and serene bedroom layouts. Their posts celebrate cozy corners and artfully curated shelving vignettes.

## 13. @serenaandlily

[Serena & Lily](https://www.serenaandlily.com) embodies casual beachside elegance. From crisp woven dining chairs to serene blue-and-white bedroom textiles, their signature coastal aesthetic brings relaxed sophistication to every corner of the house.

## 14. @onekingslane

One Kings Lane features traditional antiques, designer furnishings, and historic home restorations. Their feed is ideal for anyone who appreciates classic architecture, artwork displays, and vintage decor accents.

## 15. @amberinteriors

Amber Lewis of Amber Interiors popularized the iconic California Cool aesthetic. With layered vintage rugs, warm leather accents, and organic textures, her designs strike the perfect balance between relaxed and high-end.

## 16. @1stdibs

1stdibs showcases rare antique furniture, fine art, and high-design architectural spaces from around the globe. It is the ultimate source of high-end inspiration for serious collectors and design enthusiasts.

## 17. @em_henderson

Emily Henderson brings approachable DIY spirit, practical design tips, and playful family-friendly interiors. Her realistic styling guides break down how to style coffee tables, mantlepieces, and entryways effortlessly.

## 18. @elledecor

Elle Decor offers a window into luxury home tours, world-renowned interior designer projects, and cutting-edge design trends. Follow them to stay ahead of color trends and decor innovations.

## 19. @homepolish

Homepolish focuses on custom residential styling with clean modern accents. Their curated interior photography highlights statement wall art, ambient lighting design, and harmonious color palettes.

## 20. @frenchandfrenchinteriors

French & French Interiors brings bold pattern mixing, artistic wallpapers, and rich color combinations to life. Their expressive design philosophy proves that personal home style should always be adventurous.

## 21. @apartmenttherapy

[Apartment Therapy](https://www.apartmenttherapy.com) is the go-to resource for small-space solutions, real home tours, and budget decorating ideas. Their posts show real-life homes packed with personality and inventive storage solutions.

## 22. @studiomcgee

[Studio McGee](https://www.studiomcgee.com) has revolutionized modern traditional interior design. Shea McGee feed offers endless inspiration for styling entryways, kitchens, and cozy living rooms with timeless elegance.

## 23. @ladolcevitablog

La Dolce Vita Blog celebrates sophisticated interior design, classic architecture, and curated lifestyle aesthetics. It provides the perfect finishing touch of inspiration for anyone looking to refine their personal decor style.

To wrap up your home refresh, remember these essential styling principles:

- **Anchor with Neutral Base Furniture:** Choose timeless sofas and dining tables, then switch up accessories seasonally.
- **Layer Lighting:** Combine overhead fixtures with floor lamps and table lamps to create warm ambience.
- **Add Personal Touches:** Display travel souvenirs, gallery walls, and books to make your home uniquely yours.
