# 9 Creative Ways to Use Up Scraps of Wool and Leftover Yarn

Are you looking for some creative wool projects to use up scraps of wool and leftover yarn? Any keen knitter or crochet fan will soon accumulate a basket or box of odds and ends. Wondering what to do with scraps of yarn instead of letting them sit forgotten? Traditionally these were used to make blankets, but if you are looking for fresh, creative ways to use up yarn scraps, there are endless options. Try these 9 fun wool craft projects to transform your tiny yarn scraps into charming creations!

Here is what you will need for most of these quick projects:

- Small yarn scraps (even short odds and ends)

- Matching knitting needles or crochet hooks

- Darning needle and embroidery scissors

## 1 Juggling Cats

![Juggling Cats](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/2013/12/26194946_6302_400x300.jpg)

These cute little cats are a great way to use up scraps of wool. When pondering what to do with tiny yarn scraps, mini toys like these are ideal because even the shortest lengths can be used for whiskers and details. You can make the cats in all kinds of color combinations. If your cat loves to bat toys around, make a fun playmate for him or her.

## 2 Flower

![Flower](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/2013/12/26195105_8557_600x450.jpg)

I've made these flowers to use up my own scraps of wool. With some lovely glittery yarn left over from previous wool craft projects, I crafted eye-catching flower brooches. I only wanted a single flower, but there are also tutorials for double flowers. They would also look good as an embellishment on a bag, sweater, or paired with [cosy handmade slippers](#4).

## 3 Butterfly

![Butterfly](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/2013/12/26195145_5370_600x400.jpg)

Do you want to make something colorful with your leftover yarn? These bright butterflies give you the chance to be creative with your color scheme when searching for creative ways to use up yarn scraps. They look delightful hanging in a sunny window, or you could fasten them to a thin wooden dowel and place them in a potted plant.

## 4 Slippers

![Slippers](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/2013/12/26195216_5341_600x450.jpg)

Do your feet get chilly in winter? Warm them up with super cosy slippers. Find a pattern that lends itself to an infinite combination of colors, offering the perfect opportunity to turn your yarn scraps projects into practical footwear. You can discover free pattern guides and community tips on [Ravelry](https://www.ravelry.com). You could make them matching (or as near as possible), or create a delightfully mismatched pair.

| Project | Difficulty | Scrap Size Needed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Scrappy Slippers | Easy | Medium skein ends |
| Knitted Bookmarks | Beginner | Small yarn lengths |
| Playful Pom Poms | Beginner | Tiny odds & ends |

## 5 Finger Puppets

![Finger Puppets](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/2013/12/26195239_0179_599x442.jpg)

Finger puppets never stop being fun! Make some for your youngsters to play with; they can put on a puppet show for the whole family. They are incredibly easy to make, requiring only basic garter and stocking stitches. If you enjoy quick items like this, you might also like making [custom yarn bookmarks](#6).

## 6 Bookmark

![Bookmark](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/2013/12/26195348_8922_600x485.jpg)

Bookmarks are always useful, and this pretty version makes an ideal gift. If you have never tackled anything beyond simple stitches, look for basic guides on [Lion Brand Yarn](https://www.lionbrand.com) to help master beginner techniques. Just use a row counter to keep track of your pattern rows easily.

## 7 Angel

![Angel](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/2013/12/26195417_2523_600x420.jpg)

Wouldn't these look great hanging on a Christmas tree? It is never too early to start preparing holiday decor using leftover wool scraps. These angels look stunning in cream or pastels, though vivid jewel tones work just as nicely if you prefer a bold look.

## 8 Bunting

![Bunting](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/2013/12/26195518_8709_600x463.jpg)

If you have mastered decreasing, give this colorful bunting project a try. It takes a little time to knit enough flag triangles to stitch together, making it one of the best ways to use wool odds and ends from larger skeins. For tutorials on joining techniques and decrease stitches, check out [Yarnspirations](https://www.yarnspirations.com). Simply cast on a few stitches and knit a long header strip to bind all your flags together.

## 9 Pom Poms

![Pom Poms](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/2013/12/26195556_7270_600x450.jpg)

Finally, why not make some pom poms? They are wonderfully versatile for crafts—attach them to hats, scarves, or even accent your [festive yarn bunting](#8). You can stick to a single shade or blend multiple colors into one fluff ball.

These fun little projects will help you find a use for all those odds and ends of yarn sitting in your craft basket. Kids love helping with pom poms, though supervision with scissors is recommended. What are your favorite crafts, and what do you usually make with your leftover wool scraps?
