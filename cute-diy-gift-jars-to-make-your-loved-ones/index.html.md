# 9 Cute DIY Gift Jars to Make Your Loved Ones \(Easy Homemade Gift Ideas\)

If a loved one’s birthday, anniversary, or special holiday is coming up and you’re feeling extra crafty, how about creating thoughtful **DIY gift jars** for them? You can make them as simple or as complex as you’d like, and each and every one has the potential to be completely stunning. Make up some adorable labels, channel your inner crafty chick, and make some cute homemade gift jars that show how much you care!

| Gift Jar Type | Best Occasion | Popular Filling Ideas |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pamper Jar | Birthdays & Self-Care | Mini lotions, nail polish, lip balm |
| Date Night Jar | Anniversaries & Valentine's | Popsicle stick activity prompts |
| Message Jar | Farewell & Relationships | Handwritten notes & daily quotes |

## 1. Pamper Jar

![Pamper Jar](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/i5/vb/w4in77fe_600x900.jpg)

If you’ve got a girlfriend who’s down in the dumps, or perhaps someone you love is a mother-to-be, then this is definitely one of the DIY gift jars perfect for them. Just get a large jar and fill it with all kinds of pamper-worthy items—nail polish, nail files, rich hand cream, sheet masks, and lip gloss. You can easily find budget-friendly self-care supplies at stores like [Michaels](https://www.michaels.com). They’ll love taking some relaxing time out for themselves! Pair it with a relaxing [homemade candle jar](#9) for the ultimate cozy spa gift package.

## 2. New Baby Jar

![New Baby Jar](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/qy/om/aw6v9nq2_600x900.jpg)

A cute jar stuffed with soft bibs, a pacifier, baby lotion, and an adorable tiny singlet is the perfect gift for someone in your life that has just had a new baby. It shows you’ve put time and thought into the gift, and it’s full of practical cute things to put in a jar that parents can use regularly during those busy first months.

## 3. Cookie Jar

![Cookie Jar](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/p2/u0/gswlg56l_519x726.jpg)

Filling a jar with dry ingredients for a delicious batch of cookies will create a gift that will satisfy anyone’s sweet tooth. Whether it’s classic chocolate chip or raisin and oatmeal cookies, just layer the ingredients neatly and add a label with baking instructions telling them what wet ingredients to add and how long to cook them. It is truly the sweetest present ever!

## 4. Date Night Jar

![Date Night Jar](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/eo/x7/cx9g0r5j_600x900.jpg)

I gave my boyfriend a jar of date night ideas for Valentine’s Day this year and it was a huge hit! Write your favorite date night ideas on wooden craft sticks (or color-coded paddle pop sticks) and put them in a mason jar sealed with a pretty ribbon. Whenever you both have a free evening, pull one out for a random, wonderful date. If you love romantic gifts, you can also combine this with [message jar ideas](#7) for a sweet surprise.

- Color-code sticks by activity type (staying in vs. going out).
- Include budget-friendly dates alongside special splurge ideas.

## 5. Terrarium Jar

![Terrarium Jar](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/k6/ud/fpfxe8r1_600x862.jpg)

Terrariums are a wonderful way to bring natural beauty indoors, and making one inside a glass jar is so lovely! Just follow standard terrarium steps using small pebbles, activated charcoal, potting soil, and hardy succulents or moss. This gift makes the perfect accompaniment to any household, so it works perfectly as a sweet housewarming present.

## 6. Inspiration Jar

![Inspiration Jar](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/yx/hf/b4h6xg13_600x800.jpg)

Write inspirational messages, uplifting quotes, and favorite memories on small pieces of colorful paper and place them in your jar. When the jar is full, give it to someone in your life who needs a little boost of inspiration—I have no doubt they will appreciate it more than you know. You can find creative labels and craft tags on [Etsy](https://www.etsy.com) to give it a custom finish. Much like [date night jars](#4), it offers a personal touch that lasts long after it is opened.

## 7. Message Jar

![Message Jar](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/yt/uf/d23fajws_429x600.jpg)

If you have a friend or partner who is going away, whether it be moving or heading out on a long holiday, they will definitely appreciate a diy message jar. You can create a jar with notes for your boyfriend or best friend, filling it with daily notes, loving memories, or a heartfelt long letter. Wrap a beautiful ribbon around the lid for a perfect keepsake they will treasure forever.

## 8. Sewing Jar

![Sewing Jar](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/r9/ro/yodf5ncl_600x845.jpg)

Are you friends with a crafty person looking for a new hobby? How about making them a sewing jar kit? Just fill a jar with colorful spools of thread, needles, measuring tape, zippers, and pins—everything they need to start on a new sewing journey. You could also include a cute, simple beginner pattern to make it extra special!

## 9. Candle Jar

![Candle Jar](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/go/sa/qi1v7e9l_529x741.jpg)

If your local craft shop has candle wax and wicks, a handmade candle jar might be the ultimate cozy gift! Just melt down the wax according to package instructions, stir in your favorite essential oils like lavender or vanilla, and pour into your jar around the wick. For more creative craft ideas, browse trending designs on [Pinterest](https://www.pinterest.com) before making your next batch of homemade gift jars.

Jars are not only super-adorable, but they’re also incredibly convenient for creating personalized gifts! Homemade DIY gift jars are a wonderful way to craft meaningful, beautiful presents that look amazing without breaking the bank. Have you made gifts in a jar before? What did you make?
