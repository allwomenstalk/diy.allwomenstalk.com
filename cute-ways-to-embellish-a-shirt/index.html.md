# 10 Easy Ways to Embellish a Shirt and Dress Up Plain T\-Shirts

If you are looking for creative ways to give an old top a stylish makeover, these easy shirt embellishment ideas will help you transform basic clothes into statement pieces. Learning how to dress up a t-shirt or how to embellish a shirt does not require expensive materials or expert sewing skills. Not only are these craft ideas simple and fun, but they are also very gentle on your budget. From minimalist chic to edgy streetwear, exploring clothing embellishments ideas allows you to customize your wardrobe with personal flair. Let these DIY embellished t-shirts show you how easily you can decorate t-shirts at home.

## 1. Studs and Rhinestones

![Studs and Rhinestones](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/diy/2013/06/1_studs-and-rhinestones_600x399.jpg)

This is one of the simpler ways to embellish a shirt and instantly elevate a basic outfit. Using metallic studs, sparkling rhinestones, and accent beads, you can give a plain top a vibrant, new life. Simply press or hand-sew them onto the shoulders, collar, or pocket area for an eye-catching touch. You can grab affordable stud sets and gem setters from craft supply retailers like [Michaels](https://www.michaels.com) to get started right away.

## 2. Ribbon

![Ribbon](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/diy/2013/06/2_ribbon_600x399.jpg)

The combination of soft gray and violet ribbon adds an elegant, boutique-inspired touch to a basic crewneck tee. To create this layered trim, cut ribbons in your choice of colors, press them crisp with an iron, and hand-sew them along the neckline in overlapping layers for a customized designer look.

Before starting your t-shirt embellishments, it helps to gather a few essential craft supplies:

- Fabric glue or fusible interfacing
- Sharp fabric shears
- Clear embroidery thread and fine hand-sewing needles

## 3. Tassel and Chains

![Tassel and Chains](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/diy/2013/06/3_tassel-and-chains_503x598.jpg)

If you want a bold statement piece, combining fabric tassels and metallic chains makes a striking embellished tee. For this project, cut strips of fabric matching your top's fabric and tone, create small eyelet holes along the shoulder seams, insert the strips, and drape metal chain links alongside them. Secure everything neatly by hand sewing on the inside seam.

## 4. Lace

![Lace](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/diy/2013/06/4_lace_480x640.jpg)

Adding lace is one of the easiest ways to embellish a plain top with feminine charm. Simply trim a section of floral or geometric lace appliqué and attach it across one shoulder or sleeve. Using clear embroidery thread is recommended when hand sewing to keep the stitches completely invisible on knit fabric. You can browse fabric options at [Joann Fabrics](https://www.joann.com) to find lace trims that match your personal style.

## 5. Doily

![Doily](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/diy/2013/06/5_doily_236x354.jpg)

Vintage crochet doilies bring a romantic peek-a-boo accent to the back of a casual shirt. For this DIY project, pin and sew a decorative vintage doily onto the upper back of your tee. Carefully cut away the original shirt fabric behind the doily, leaving a delicate openwork panel similar to lace cut-out techniques.

## 6. Cut-out Lace

![Cut-out Lace](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/diy/2013/06/6_cut-out-lace_464x640.jpg)

This technique builds upon lace embellishments by combining lace appliqués with fabric cutouts. Sew wide lace trim along the neckline or yoke of your plain top, then carefully trim away the cotton layer beneath to create sheer lace paneling. Make sure to use a ballpoint jersey needle if machine sewing stretchy knits to prevent skipped stitches.

## 7. Sequins

![Sequins](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/diy/2013/06/7_sequins_435x582.jpg)

You do not have to limit shirt diy ideas to the neckline or back panel. Transforming the sleeves with shimmering sequined fabric is a chic way to dress up a plain top. Measure and cut sequin trim or fabric panels to fit the sleeves, then secure them around the arm cuffs using sturdy hand stitching.

Here is a quick overview comparing these clothing embellishment techniques to help you choose your next project:

| Embellishment Technique | Skill Level | Estimated Time |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Studs & Rhinestones | Beginner | 15–20 mins |
| Lace Cut-outs | Intermediate | 30–45 mins |
| Celtic Knot Shoulders | Intermediate | 40–60 mins |

## 8. Painted Flowers

![Painted Flowers](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/diy/2013/06/8_painted-flowers_500x380.jpg)

Hand-painted floral motifs give any plain top a custom boutique appearance. If freehand fabric painting feels daunting, you can achieve the exact same look by using pre-printed floral fabric. Simply cut out your favorite flower patterns, iron them onto double-sided fusible interfacing, press them onto your shirt, and finish with a clean edge stitch.

## 9. Feathers, Gems, and Rosette Trim

![Feathers, Gems, and Rosette Trim](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/diy/2013/06/9_feathers-gems-and-rosette-trim_600x867.jpg)

Designed by craft expert Erica Domesek of [P.S. I Made This](https://psimadethis.com), this dramatic design uses lightweight shoulder pads as the sturdy foundation for an elaborate mix of feathers, fabric rosettes, and sparkling rhinestones and gems. The structured shoulder pad base gives your shirt a high-fashion, broad-shouldered silhouette while supporting the weight of rich embellishments.

## 10. Knot

![Knot](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/diy/2013/06/10_knot_236x176.jpg)

Braided fabric details and nautical rope accents offer a fresh way to cut shirt sleeve ideas and revamp plain necklines. By weaving cotton trim into Celtic triangle knots along the shoulder seams, you can create intricate structural details that transform a simple shirt into a custom masterpiece.

Which of these cute ways to embellish a shirt are you excited to try first? Whether you choose bold studs or delicate floral accents, upcycling plain shirts is a satisfying and budget-friendly way to refresh your style.
