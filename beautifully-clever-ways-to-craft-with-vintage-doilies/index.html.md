# 7 Creative Things to Do with Vintage Doilies: Easy DIY Crafts & Decor Ideas

Vintage doilies often bring to mind the musty, nostalgia-filled atmosphere of a grandparent's attic or flea market bins. You might initially wrinkle your nose at how old-fashioned they appear, but these delicate lace pieces actually make wonderful craft materials for modern homes. If you have inherited a collection of grandma's doilies and find yourself wondering what to do with doilies, you are in luck. While many wonder are doilies worth anything, their real value lies in their potential for creative art with doilies and charming home accents. From functional decor to wearable fashion, there are endless creative things to do with doilies. Here are seven brilliant ideas for doilies to start upcycling today:

| Doily Craft Project | Essential Materials | Difficulty Level |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Doily Pendant Lamp | Vintage doilies, balloon, Mod Podge, light kit | Easy |
| Doily Luminaries | Glass jar, spray adhesive, doily, tealight | Beginner |
| Doily Dreamcatcher | Embroidery hoop, lace doily, twine, beads | Beginner |

## 1. Turn Doilies into a Pendant Lamp

![Turn Doilies into a Pendant Lamp](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/n3/ss/empg4t5z5542c48fb49e6803102552_425x640.jpg)

This DIY doily pendant lamp or doily lantern is so strikingly pretty that it instantly elevates any room's decor. Beyond its obvious aesthetic appeal, this intricate lighting project is surprisingly simple to create at home. The process involves saturating vintage doilies in a stiffening solution, wrapping them around an inflated balloon, and letting them dry thoroughly before popping the balloon. Once dried, insert a standard pendant light cord set through the top opening for a stunning, ambient light fixture. For a detailed walkthrough, check out the original [doily lamp tutorial](http://emmmylizzzy.blogspot.com/2012/04/doily-lamp-tutorial-finally.html).

Key steps for building a doily lamp:

- Choose cotton vintage doilies with open lace patterns for optimal light diffusion.
- Coat doilies generously with diluted fabric stiffener or Mod Podge.
- Overlap edges slightly over a round balloon to form a sturdy spherical shell.

## 2. Make a Doily Bowl

![Make a Doily Bowl](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/a9/sh/g0bd995n5542c494cd5c7413940119_600x804.jpg)

There is something about a vintage doily that immediately screams gorgeous, no matter what project you end up creating with it. Using a shaping technique similar to the [pendant lamp](#1) project above, you can turn a flat piece of lace into a delicate catch-all bowl. Mold your stiffened doily over the bottom of an inverted glass bowl or balloon to achieve the perfect structural shape. Once hardened, it serves as an elegant holder for jewelry, keys, or potpourri on your entryway table.

## 3. Give a Tank Top a Doily Makeover

![Give a Tank Top a Doily Makeover](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/vl/i1/hwzn28u85542c495b57cd280422392_600x800.jpg)

Got an old tank top in your closet that could use a fresh lease on life? Give it a lovely doily treatment for a chic, bohemian makeover. This simple sewing project typically takes only half an hour to complete. You can stitch a large vintage doily onto the back panel of a plain top, or add tiny doilies to adorn the shoulders and sleeves of a cardigan or favorite blouse. Exploring [upcycled clothing crafts](https://diy.allwomenstalk.com/) like this is a budget-friendly way to refresh your wardrobe.

## 4. Make Lovely Doily Luminaries

![Make Lovely Doily Luminaries](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/rz/sq/jsjp81l55542c4969d871234819781_400x568.jpg)

Crafting doily luminaries is perhaps one of the most popular and satisfying ways to craft with doilies. To create these dainty candle holders for your home or patio, simply adhere a lace doily around a clean glass jar using spray adhesive or Mod Podge. Once dry, wrap the jar rim with burlap or satin ribbon and pop a battery-powered LED tealight candle inside. When darkness falls, the flame casts mesmerizing lace shadows across your space. See the full step-by-step guide at [Crafts by Amanda](https://craftsbyamanda.com/burlap-doily-luminaries-rustic-meets/).

## 5. Create a Gorgeous Doily Necklace

![Create a Gorgeous Doily Necklace](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/kd/rs/qwr2nr8z5542c4984fb46441087096_600x786.jpg)

Transforming small lace doilies into wearable statement jewelry is a fantastic project for fashion enthusiasts. By applying fabric stiffener or liquid starch to miniature doilies, you can turn flexible lace into a structured bib-style necklace. Connect jewelry jump rings and a delicate chain to the edges to complete the piece. It is a creative way to display intricate vintage needlework as a one-of-a-kind accessory. If you love handmade accessories, explore more [DIY jewelry ideas](https://diy.allwomenstalk.com/) for extra inspiration.

## 6. Stretch a Doily for Jewelry Display

![Stretch a Doily for Jewelry Display](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/d2/ps/os2xooxi5542c497e3817153879033_600x800.jpg)

This vintage doily project requires no complex craft skills or sewing experience. Simply secure a round lace doily inside a wooden embroidery hoop, pull it taut, and trim any excess fabric. The open mesh pattern of the lace provides convenient tiny holes to hang and display your favorite drop earrings. You can attach a satin ribbon to the top screw mechanism to hang your new wall-mounted jewelry organizer in your bedroom or dressing area.

Quick display ideas for framed lace:

- Paint the wooden embroidery hoop in gold or pastel tones for added contrast.
- Group multiple hoops of varying sizes together to create a wall art gallery.
- Use central lace openings to pin brooches or hook dangling necklaces.

## 7. Catch Dreams with a Vintage Doily

![Catch Dreams with a Vintage Doily](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/j1/uz/pjvgykl05542c49820341767442734_600x892.jpg)

Here is another beloved DIY idea for old doilies: making a boho-chic dreamcatcher. Instead of weaving a traditional web, center a vintage lace doily inside an embroidery hoop or circular wire ring wrapped in twine. Fasten the edges securely with yarn or thread, and attach long cascading ribbons, wooden beads, and feathers to the bottom rim. Much like the [jewelry display hoop](#6), this project turns vintage textiles into eye-catching wall decor.

The next time you visit a local flea market or unearth a box of old doilies at your grandma's house, do not let them gather dust. Whether you craft a glowing doily lantern or a romantic piece of wall art, these timeless textiles offer endless inspiration for DIY doilies projects. Grab your craft glue and start experimenting with these vintage crafts today!
