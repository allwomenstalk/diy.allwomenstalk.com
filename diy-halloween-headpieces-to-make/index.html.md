# 12 Easy DIY Halloween Headpieces to Make for Any Costume

We have already covered masks, but our journey into Halloween crafts is far from over! Today, we are exploring the best **DIY Halloween headpieces** to elevate your spooky style. If you are not planning on covering your full face this Halloween but still want a show-stopping piece to wear from the neck up, learning **how to make your own headpiece for a costume** is the ultimate solution. Just like our [mask](https://diy.allwomenstalk.com/diy-halloween-masks-to-make/) and [crown](https://diy.allwomenstalk.com/totally-cute-diy-crowns/) lists, there is something in these creative **halloween headpiece diy** ideas for all types of costumes and for revelers of all ages.

| Headpiece Style | Primary Materials | Difficulty Level |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Feather Headdress | Colorful ribbons, craft feathers | Easy |
| Cardboard Skyline | Corrugated cardboard, paint | Easy |
| Medusa Snakes | Plastic snakes, braid extensions | Medium |

## 1. Feathers

![Feathers](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/diy/2012/10/12_400x267.jpg)

Using colorful ribbon, faux flowers, and craft feathers, you can assemble a vibrant **DIY Halloween headpiece** for a Boho princess or festival-inspired look. The combination of textures makes this **headdress for halloween** stand out in any crowd. Don't you just love the rich colors of the ribbons used here? The glittery accent ribbon adds a playful sparkle that catches the light beautifully.

[Tutorial](http://isthereabox.blogspot.com/2012/04/diy-feather-headdress.html)

## 2. Skyline

![Skyline](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/diy/2012/10/23_450x300.jpg)

How cute! As you can see, this is a fantastic **halloween headpiece idea** for the entire family. The primary material needed for these clever headpieces is simple corrugated cardboard. You cut the cardboard outlining your favorite city skyline or building top, paint in window and architectural details, and attach an elastic band for tying comfortably around your head.

[Tutorial](https://www.marthastewart.com/265871/all-dressed-up-skyline)

## 3. Toadstool

![Toadstool](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/diy/2012/10/33_420x317.jpg)

This mushroom-themed **diy costume head piece** is all sorts of adorable! While the model in the photo is a little girl, you can easily adapt this whimsical design for adults as well. The simple solution is to use a wide-brimmed felt hat or beret as your sturdy base before adding felt toadstool spots.

[Tutorial](https://goo.gl/EEtY9o)

## 4. Zipped

![Zipped](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/diy/2012/10/43_400x306.jpg)

High-fashion meets avant-garde Halloween craftiness with this runway-inspired zipper crown. While it might take a moment to figure out which costume best pairs with this unique **diy headpiece**, its creative use of unzipped metallic zippers makes it far too stylish to omit.

[Tutorial](http://www.monoxious.com/diy-fashion-tutorial-ann-demeulemeester-ss2010-zipper-headdress/)

## 5. Bat

![Bat](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/diy/2012/10/53-560x417_560x417.jpg)

This floating bat headband is a classic staple when learning **how to make a halloween headpiece**. To craft this dramatic look, gather these basic supplies:

- Black cardstock or stiff felt
- Craft wire or floral wire
- Hot glue gun
- A thin black headband

Simply cut out bat silhouettes from black paper, secure each bat to a piece of craft wire, and twist the opposite end of the wire around the headband base. Add as many floating bats as you like for a swarm effect!

[Tutorial](https://www.cutoutandkeep.net/projects/cute-bat-in-your-hair)

## 6. Furry Ears

![Furry Ears](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/diy/2012/10/63_500x350.jpg)

Soft and charming, this design remains a top pick among quick **DIY Halloween headpieces**. They are incredibly easy to make with faux fur scraps, headband bases, and basic craft tools. You can customize the ear shape for cats, wolves, or bears in under 30 minutes.

[Tutorial](http://www.gleefulthings.com/blog/?p=2883)

## 7. Bunny Ears

![Bunny Ears](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/diy/2012/10/73_450x290.jpg)

These lace bunny ears bring a touch of vintage glamour to your outfit. Inspired by designer runway accessories, this lace-covered wire pair proves that timeless lace accessories always look sophisticated on Halloween night.

[Tutorial](http://www.behindseams.com/2009/09/diy-maison-michels-laced-bunny-ears.html)

## 8. Medusa

![Medusa](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/diy/2012/10/83_450x300.jpg)

For a mythical and chilling look, a **medusa headpiece diy** project is hard to beat! To recreate this snake-entwined hairstyle, you will need braided hair extensions, sturdy bobby pins, and lightweight plastic toy snakes. If the snakes tend to slip out, discreetly thread dark string or wire to anchor them securely to your braids.

[Tutorial](https://www.marthastewart.com/271526/medusa-hair)

## 9. Rapunzel

![Rapunzel](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/diy/2012/10/92_400x334.jpg)

Transform into fairytale royalty with lush floor-length yarn braids! To adapt this cozy hairpiece for Halloween, gather plenty of golden or colorful yarn, a pair of tights to form the comfortable base skullcap, and silk flowers to weave into the finished braid.

[Tutorial](https://www.marthastewart.com)

## 10. Rose

![Rose](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/diy/2012/10/102_360x261.jpg)

Oversized floral headwear adds instant romantic drama to any costume. Perfect as a botanical **halloween headdress** or flower fairy crown, this giant rose topper can easily be adapted for theatrical costumes or spring pageants long after October 31st.

[Tutorial](http://www.marthastewart.com?ref=allwomenstalk.com)

## 11. Bat II

![Bat II](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/diy/2012/10/111-560x420_560x420.jpg)

Here is another clever bat-inspired **DIY Halloween headpiece** idea. Rather than bouncing wire bats, this sleek variation features a sculpted bat silhouette arranged like a sophisticated hair bow, complete with tiny white felt fangs for a cute yet gothic accent.

[Tutorial](https://www.cutoutandkeep.net/projects/cute-bat-in-your-hair)

## 12. Animal Hats

![Animal Hats](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/diy/2012/10/121_450x300.jpg)

Rounding out our roundup of **headpiece ideas**, this comprehensive fleece tutorial shows you how to craft five distinct cozy animal hats. Downloadable printable patterns make sizing simple whether you are sewing for kids or adults.

[Tutorial](https://www.fleecefun.com/halloween-hat-pack/)

We hope you enjoyed this handpicked collection of creative **DIY Halloween headpieces**! Whether you opt for floating bats, a regal Medusa snake braid, or simple furry ears, making your own headwear brings personalized magic to any Halloween costume. Which project will you be crafting this year?
