# 10 Stunning Silver DIY Projects to Elevate Your Home Decor

If you want your home to have a touch of class, adding some of these silver DIY projects is just the ticket. When I say **silver DIYs**, I am talking about crafts that have been enhanced with lovely detailing in this metallic shade; these do not refer to silverware or silver jewelry projects. Let's reserve those types of crafts for another day. For now, let's take a look at these **stunning DIY silver crafts** and explore what to do with silver accents to transform your space on a budget.

## 1. Dresser

![Dresser](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/diy/2012/09/16_550x413.jpg)

A dresser in a metallic silver color totally screams chic and elegant. You can go about making a silver dresser using metallic spray paint or acrylic furniture paint, or you can do what Jenny did. She used [silver leaf sheets](https://www.speedballart.com/) to cover a dresser she got on clearance. Silver leafing gives a rich, layered texture that paint alone cannot replicate. You're going to want to check out the tutorial to see the original look of this now-classy piece of furniture.

[Tutorial](http://littlegreennotebook.blogspot.com/2009/12/silver-leaf-dressers.html)

## 2. Seashells

![Seashells](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/diy/2012/09/24-560x373_560x373.jpg)

Seashells painted in metallic colors are always beautiful additions to coastal or modern home decor. You can go for gold or copper tones, but silver brings a crisp, sophisticated shine. The best product for painting shells silver is [Krylon Chrome Spray Paint](https://www.krylon.com/), according to Becca. Display them in decorative glass bowls or use them as place card holders for a dinner party. Give it a try!

[Tutorial](https://www.fromgardners2bergers.com/2011/05/silver-seashells-down-by-seashore.html)

## 3. Lamp Shade

![Lamp Shade](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/diy/2012/09/34_500x375.jpg)

This **silver DIY project** requires covering the interior of a basic lamp shade with silver leafing. When the bulb is turned on, the metallic interior reflects a warm, shimmering glow throughout the room, creating an upscale designer look. The result is something surprisingly chic and warm. Head on over to Design*Sponge for the full step-by-step tutorial.

Here are a few quick tips for leafing a lamp shade:

- Apply metal leaf adhesive sizing evenly before laying down the sheets.
- Use a soft bristle brush to burnish the silver leaf smoothly.
- Seal with a clear topcoat to prevent tarnishing over time.

[Tutorial](https://www.designsponge.com/2012/05/diy-project-silver-leafed-lampshade.html)

## 4. Chair

![Chair](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/diy/2012/09/43_450x380.jpg)

Not only did Suesan give this accent chair a striking metallic finish, but she also took the project a step further by giving the furniture a classic antiqued treatment. She began by applying several coats of [Rust-Oleum metallic spray paint](https://www.rustoleum.com/). Next, she applied a dark antiquing glaze into the carved grooves and sealed everything with clear furniture wax. The result is a beautifully faux-aged silver chair that looks like a vintage find.

[Tutorial](http://froufrugal.blogspot.com/2011/06/aged-silver-finish.html)

## 5. Wall

![Wall](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/diy/2012/09/54_478x359.jpg)

If you think **DIY silver projects** are only comprised of small crafts or furniture makeovers, think again! You can also give an entire section of your room a brilliant metallic finish. For example, covering a focal wall with heavy-duty foil or textured silver wallpaper brings an industrial, shimmering charm to your living space. Pairing a metallic accent wall with a [silver leaf dresser](#1) creates a cohesive, modern aesthetic.

[Tutorial](http://theivycottageblog.blogspot.com/2011/06/aluminum-foil-wall-covering.html)

## 6. Straw Accessories

![Straw Accessories](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/diy/2012/09/64_450x320.jpg)

One effortless way to refresh plain straw baskets, woven placemats, or tote bags is to spray paint them with metallic silver paint. This budget-friendly craft takes less than an hour to complete. Much like refinishing an [antiqued silver chair](#4), spray painting textured natural materials gives them an instant modern upgrade. Spray, let dry, seal with a clear coat, and you're done!

## 7. Hearts

![Hearts](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/diy/2012/09/7a-560x377_560x377.jpg)

These charming decorative hearts are sculpted from simple salt dough and finished with metallic spray paint. They look surprisingly similar to real sterling silver keepsakes. To make the salt dough at home, combine 1 cup flour, 1/2 cup salt, and 1/2 cup water. Mix the dough, roll and cut the heart shapes, bake until hardened, sand smooth, and apply your metallic coat.

## 8. Mod Art

![Mod Art](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/diy/2012/09/84_547x410.jpg)

These shiny geometric circles are crafted from silver leaf and mounted inside a sleek dark frame for a high-end modern art look. To recreate this art piece, apply silver leaf onto sturdy cardstock, cut out neat circles, and arrange them on a contrasting mat board before framing. It is an accessible way to make stunning wall art without spending a fortune.

[Tutorial](https://www.midwesterngirldiy.com/2012/05/step-outside-your-comfort-zonewith.html)

## 9. Pumpkins

![Pumpkins](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/diy/2012/09/94_400x300.jpg)

Perfect for autumn celebrations like Halloween and Thanksgiving, these gilded pumpkins bring a glam touch to seasonal decorating. Gather faux craft pumpkins, base paint, and silver leaf sheets. Once you apply the leafing to the outer ridges, set your gleaming gourds on a fireplace mantel or dining table centerpiece to enjoy throughout the holidays.

## 10. Tray

![Tray](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/diy/2012/09/103_475x340.jpg)

Another versatile household item you can elevate with metallic accents is a wooden or metal serving tray. Adding botanical stencils or leafed detailing transforms an ordinary tray into an elegant centerpiece. Much like creating custom [framed silver leaf mod art](#8), detailing a tray adds instant luxury to coffee tables and vanity counters.
