# 7 Best Online Photography Courses Review for Happy Snappers & Beginners

There is a wealth of online photography courses available today for photographers of every level. Whether you want to learn how to take better pictures with your smartphone, master practical ways to zjoosh up your pictures, take up creative photography as a fulfilling hobby, or explore specialized skills like underwater shooting or a talk to camera course, you can easily find structured lessons online. Modern online photography learning platforms offer flexible options that replaced the old-fashioned photography correspondence course format, giving happy snappers access to world-class instruction right from home.

## 1. PhotographyCourse.net

This website offers one of the biggest ranges of online photography courses on the net, catering to all skill levels. There is no pre-required expertise or experience criteria to start learning from [PhotographyCourse.net](https://photographycourse.net) because they take you on at any skill level and allow you to learn up to a rather advanced stage. The more you learn within the course and the more you practice as you go, the higher the level of material you are able to access. Readers searching for a comprehensive photographycourse.net review will appreciate how step-by-step each module feels for beginners.

## 2. BetterPhoto

This is a course that gives you a foundation level amount of teaching so that you may get started and get practicing right away. There are more than one hundred lessons within the course where you first improve your photography skills learning the basics, and then keep on learning until you become an advanced photographer. You do have to pay money to gain access to the courses, and they vary in length, content, and the amount of time you need to invest. If you prefer structured lessons with feedback similar to [Digital Photography School](#3), this remains a classic option to explore.

| Platform | Skill Level | Best Feature |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| PhotographyCourse.net | Beginner to Advanced | Free structured step-by-step modules |
| Digital Photography School | All Levels | Practical shooting tutorials & guides |
| DPReview | Intermediate to Expert | In-depth camera and lens reviews |

## 3. Digital Photography School

This is a website that is host to a school where you learn photography online via shooting tutorials and lessons. The lessons go from simple tips and how-to instructions on using your equipment, to how to set up your shots, lighting composition, and how to improve the overall quality of your photography. The newsletter is still running and has handy weekly tips on it too, and the website marketing claims that the information given out is used by experts around the world. Anyone reading digital photography school reviews will notice how popular their step-by-step guides are for everyday snappers.

## 4. 1MillionDollarPhoto.com

There are learning sections on the website that you may pick up and learn from—with the hardest part actually being finding them across the site layout. They are there and are perfect for new learners who are just starting out and want to take shots that are as good as the ones featured on the website. It is a great website if you are interested in photography in any way and not looking for a structured course or rigid lessons. For those who want more technical gear analysis alongside creative inspiration, checking out [in-depth equipment reviews](#6) can also help elevate your skills.

## 5. Photo.net

This is a website that has a lot of information about photography. It is one of the online photography courses and community forums that has been running for a very long time. This is why it has so much information and why the website has grown to quite a large size. It allows you to do quite a lot of in-depth research into photography and you may pursue your photography skill as per your exact interests. It really has as much information as you may care to stomach, with the main challenge being that you have to navigate all of this information to find what you need. Unlike focused lighting blogs such as [Strobist](#7), Photo.net covers everything from vintage film techniques to modern digital setups.

Here are key skills you can master through online photography learning:

- Understanding manual camera settings including aperture, shutter speed, and ISO
- Mastering natural and artificial lighting setup techniques
- Post-processing and editing photos for professional color and contrast

## 6. Digital Photography Review

This is one of the very informative online photography learning resources that tells you how to take photographs, how to improve your photo quality, and how to improve your overall photography technique. The site, widely known as [DPReview](https://www.dpreview.com), also features detailed reviews on pieces of equipment and tells you which gear is the best to use for certain types of shots. It teaches you the basics and then moves on to the more advanced stuff that you may experiment with, such as what equipment to use at night and which lenses perform best in challenging weather conditions.

## 7. Strobist

Not so much a traditional course but still a great resource, this is a blog where people show off their photographs and you can apply to show off yours. They are a great aid to an offline photography course or self-guided study because you can see what other people are doing and read detailed discussions on how certain lighting setups were achieved. You can learn about how other photographers produced their shots and then apply what you have learned to your own creative work. It is a fantastic way to learn from peers, master off-camera flash, and consistently improve your skills.

There are tons of sites that can help you improve your photography skills through a best online photography courses review or hands-on practice. This guide was just a taster of what is available for passionate photographers. Are you into taking pictures with a DSLR camera, or are you content to snap away with your smartphone?
